First, the number of reactions per day (N) required for 3 GW thermal power is:
$N = \frac{P_{th} \cdot t}{E_r} = \frac{(3 \times 10^9 \, \text{J/s}) \cdot (86400 \, \text{s})}{(17.6 \times 10^6 \, \text{eV}) \cdot (1.6 \times 10^{-19} \, \text{J/eV})} \approx 9.21 \times 10^{25}$

The mass of Deuterium ($m_D$) consumed is calculated by converting reactions to moles:
$m_D = \frac{N}{N_A} \cdot M_D = \frac{9.21 \times 10^{25}}{6.0 \times 10^{23} \, \text{mol}^{-1}} \cdot (2 \, \text{g/mol}) \approx 307 \, \text{g}$

Similarly, the mass of Tritium ($m_T$) consumed per day is:
$m_T = \frac{N}{N_A} \cdot M_T = \frac{9.21 \times 10^{25}}{6.0 \times 10^{23} \, \text{mol}^{-1}} \cdot (3 \, \text{g/mol}) \approx 460 \, \text{g}$
